# Aircraft Wing Lift Calculator 

A Python-based aerospace engineering project that calculates aircraft wing lift using fundamental aerodynamics principles.

## Project Objective

The purpose of this project is to explore how wing area, air density, velocity, and lift coefficient affect aircraft lift generation.

## Lift Equation

The project uses the standard aerodynamic lift equation:

L = 0.5 × ρ × V² × S × CL

Where:

- L = Lift Force (N)
- ρ = Air Density (kg/m³)
- V = Velocity (m/s)
- S = Wing Area (m²)
- CL = Lift Coefficient

## Why This Matters

Lift is one of the fundamental forces that enables aircraft flight.

Understanding how velocity, air density, wing area, and lift coefficient influence lift generation provides valuable insight into aircraft design, performance, and aerospace engineering principles.

## Research Question

How do wing characteristics and flight conditions influence the amount of lift generated by an aircraft?

## Results

The calculator successfully computes aerodynamic lift force using the standard lift equation.

Example:

- Air Density = 1.225 kg/m³
- Velocity = 120 m/s
- Wing Area = 30 m²
- Lift Coefficient = 0.85

Calculated Lift Force:

✅ 224,910 N

The project demonstrates how changes in airspeed, wing area, and lift coefficient significantly affect lift generation.

## Engineering Insights

The analysis shows that velocity has the strongest influence on lift generation because lift increases with the square of airspeed.

The project also demonstrates how wing geometry and environmental conditions directly affect aircraft performance.

## Key Lessons Learned

- Lift is generated through the interaction of multiple variables.
- Airspeed has a significant effect on lift production.
- Mathematics provides the foundation for aerodynamics analysis.

- Small changes in flight conditions can produce large performance differences.
